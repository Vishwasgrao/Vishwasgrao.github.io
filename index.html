About Me

Senior QA Engineer with 6+ years of experience in insurance and claims platforms. Passionate about automation, quality engineering, and improving system reliability.

My QA Journey (2019 – Present)

I started my QA journey in 2019 with a simple role — executing test cases and reporting defects. But over time, I realized QA is much more than that.

From manual testing to automation, from API validation to performance testing, my journey has been about continuous learning and growth. I worked across global projects including the UK, US, Germany, and Spain, gaining strong domain expertise in insurance and claims systems.

One of the biggest shifts in my career came when I started handling UAT and production support. Working on real production issues (P0–P3 incidents) taught me how critical quality is in live systems.

Instead of just fixing issues, I began analyzing patterns — why defects occur, how they can be prevented, and how testing can be improved. This led me to strengthen automation and build better regression strategies.

As a Senior QA Engineer, I now focus on guiding automation strategy, prioritizing high-impact areas, and aligning QA efforts with business goals. I also explored AI-based defect management to improve efficiency and reporting.

Today, QA for me is about ownership, prevention, and delivering reliable systems — not just testing.
